Cᴼᴰᴱᴰᴱᴹ's Lore Book: Part Two

The powerful Codenergy is able to keep the fungus in check, bringing a stop to the war of Cᴼᴰᴱᴰᴱᴹ against the infection. Plasma AKA lightning cancels out with Codenergy, draining the levels from Cᴼᴰᴱᴰᴱᴹ.
